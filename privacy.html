Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  |  CHASE Territory

This Privacy Policy explains how CHASE Territory ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our fitness and territory-capture application.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your username, email address, gender (optional), date of birth (optional), and country/city (optional).

Location Data: The App uses your device's GPS to track your real-time position during active run or cycle sessions. Location is used to capture territory hexes and calculate distance, pace, and speed. Location data from sessions is stored to build your personal territory map.

Activity Data: We store session data including distance, duration, pace, calories, and sectors captured per session.

Profile Picture: If you choose to upload a profile picture, it is stored securely on our servers.

Device Information: We collect basic device information for security purposes, including a session key to prevent unauthorized access from multiple devices.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the core app functionality — territory capture, leaderboards, challenges
  • To display your progress, stats, and achievements
  • To show your position on city and global leaderboards
  • To enable multiplayer features such as live challenges and rival tracking
  • To detect and prevent cheating (e.g. vehicle speed detection)
  • To send in-app notifications about rival activity and challenges
  • To improve the app based on usage patterns

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your username, city, country, and territory stats are visible to other users on leaderboards and in challenge features. Your exact GPS coordinates are never shared with other users — only your territory hexes are visible on the shared map.

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage. Data is stored on servers in the Asia-Pacific region.
  • Vercel — app hosting and content delivery.
  • CartoDB / OpenStreetMap — map tile rendering (no personal data shared).

4. Location Data — Important Details

Location tracking is only active when you explicitly start a run or cycle session by tapping "Start Run". Location tracking stops when you end the session or close the app. We do not track your location in the background when the app is not in use.

Your precise GPS coordinates are processed locally on your device to calculate territory hexes. Only the resulting hex IDs (not your exact coordinates) are stored in our database.

5. Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. Session history and territory data is retained indefinitely to maintain your personal map and leaderboard position. You can request deletion of all your data by contacting us.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS), row-level security on all database tables, and session key enforcement to prevent unauthorized account access. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email below. You can also delete your account directly from within the app.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact us at: privacy@chaseterritory.app
